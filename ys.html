隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

最后更新 / Last updated：2026-09-08

01

概述 / Overview

“Efficiency Tools” (also referred to as “the Extension”) is a browser extension that lets you keep certain bookmarks private. The Extension encrypts your private bookmarks and stores them only on your own device. We do not operate any server, do not collect any personal information, and cannot access your data in any way.

「效率工具」浏览器扩展用于将你指定的书签加密隐藏。扩展只把加密后的数据保存在你自己的设备上：我们没有运营任何服务器，不收集任何个人信息，也以任何方式都无法接触到你的数据。

02

我们收集什么 / What We Collect

Nothing. The Extension does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data, browsing history, bookmark content, usage statistics, or device information. It performs no analytics and shows no ads. There is no account system, and no data ever leaves your browser.

什么都不收集。扩展不收集、不上传、不共享任何个人信息、浏览记录、书签内容、使用统计或设备信息；不包含统计组件，不展示广告，没有账号体系。没有任何数据离开你的浏览器。

03

数据如何存储 / How Your Data Is Stored

Your private bookmarks are encrypted locally with industry-standard cryptography before being written to browser storage:

你的私密书签在写入浏览器存储之前，会先使用行业标准的加密算法在本地完成加密：

  • Encryption algorithm: AES-256-GCM, with a random 12-byte IV for each write.
  • 加密算法：AES-256-GCM，每次写入使用随机 12 字节 IV。
  • Key derivation: PBKDF2-SHA256 with 310,000 iterations and a random 16-byte salt, deriving a 512-bit key (32 bytes for encryption, 32 bytes for verification).
  • 密钥派生：PBKDF2-SHA256，31 万次迭代，附加随机 16 字节盐，派生 512 位密钥（前 32 字节用于加密，后 32 字节用于校验）。
  • Only the ciphertext and a one-way verification hash are stored; your password and plaintext never appear in storage.
  • 存储中只保留密文与单向校验哈希；密码与明文从不写入存储。
  • The decryption key lives only in chrome.storage.session and is wiped automatically when the browser closes, so the private folders re-lock themselves.
  • 解密密钥仅存在于 chrome.storage.session，浏览器关闭即被自动清除，私密收藏夹随之自动上锁。
04

数据删除与密码说明 / Deletion & Password

Uninstalling the Extension removes all of its data — encrypted vault, settings, and the in-memory key — from your browser. Because the design deliberately provides no password recovery, a forgotten password cannot be reset: the data becomes unrecoverable, and the only option is to uninstall and reinstall the Extension to start fresh.

卸载扩展会从浏览器中移除其全部数据——加密数据、设置与内存中的密钥。由于设计上刻意不提供密码找回通道，忘记密码无法重置：数据将无法恢复，只能卸载并重新安装扩展后重新开始。

Security by design: without a recovery channel, no one — including us or anyone with access to your device’s storage — can decrypt your private bookmarks without your password. 安全性设计：由于不存在找回通道，任何人（包括我们或能访问你设备存储的人）在不知道密码的情况下都无法解密你的私密书签。
05

权限说明 / Permissions

The Extension requests only the permissions it genuinely needs:

扩展只申请其真正需要的权限：

  • bookmarks — used solely to create, read, and hide the private folder(s) you designate; regular bookmarks are never touched or read into the encrypted vault.
  • bookmarks — 仅用于创建、读取和隐藏你指定的私密文件夹；普通书签不会被读取或写入加密数据。
  • storage — used to save your encrypted vault and preferences locally on your device.
  • storage — 用于在你的设备本地保存加密数据与偏好设置。
06

网络与外部链接 / Network & External Links

The Extension itself makes no network requests. The popup may show a link to “AI 信息台” (aixxt.com) and a “Feedback” link; these are opened only when you click them. Once you leave the Extension, the pages you visit are governed by their own sites’ policies, not by this policy.

扩展本身不发起任何网络请求。弹窗中可能展示「AI信息台」（aixxt.com）与「意见与反馈」链接，仅在你主动点击时才会打开。离开扩展后所访问的页面，适用对应网站自身的政策，与本政策无关。

07

未成年人隐私 / Children’s Privacy

The Extension is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. Since no personal data is collected from any user — child or adult — no special handling is required beyond this notice.

本扩展为通用工具，不针对未成年人设计。由于我们不向任何用户（无论未成年人还是成年人）收集个人数据，除本声明外无需额外处理。

本政策如有更新，将同步修订页面顶部的「最后更新」日期。任何变更都只反映扩展的真实行为；「仅本地存储、零数据收集」的承诺不会被削弱。
